What Alii is

Not a chatbot. A machine.

Alii is a six-machine cluster where every box has a job. Open LLMs run on the GPU workstation; a multi-agent stack with long-term memory orchestrates everything; monitoring watches every service. You talk to it over iMessage or get hourly digest notifications — from anywhere.

Memory is the difference. Alii holds roughly 370,000 memories — conversations, projects, preferences — embedded and searchable, so it gets smarter about you every day instead of forgetting you every session.

Why local

Your data never leaves the house

Every mainstream AI product is a pipeline from your keyboard to somebody else's servers. Alii is the opposite architecture.

☁️

The cloud way

Your prompts, photos, and documents travel to data centers owned by companies that monetize attention. Subscriptions meter your usage. Outages and policy changes break your tools. You are the product being processed.

🏠

The Alii way

Models run on hardware you own, on your own network. Memories stay in your own database. No accounts, no usage meters, no one reading over your shoulder. When the internet goes down, Alii still works — it's right there.
